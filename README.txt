MEDINA BOWL — MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE

Open index.html in a browser to start.
Keep all five HTML files, styles.css and script.js together in the same folder.

Pages:
- index.html — Home and campaign hero
- story.html — Brand story, healthier-food adaptations and nutrient explanations
- menu.html — Food and drinks menu with prices
- recipe.html — Atlas Chicken Bowl ingredients, measurements and step-by-step method
- visit.html — Fictional Bondi location, hours, advertising rationale and Australian social-media statistics

The restaurant address, contact details, hours and prices are illustrative. External images and Google Fonts require an internet connection. Images are visual inspiration and should be replaced with original photos of the actual dishes for a final school/business submission.
