Reaching our audience

Why Instagram and TikTok?

Our target market is young adults aged 18–30 in Sydney who discover food through friends, creators, location tags and short-form video. Medina Bowl is highly visual: colourful ingredients and quick bowl-assembly videos can communicate the experience in seconds.

70%of personal-device time among Australian 14–24-year-olds was spent on social media, averaging 3 hours 31 minutes per day in the Ipsos iris Gen Z report (2025).
98%of Australia's online population used social media in the past month, according to Ipsos iris data reported in September 2025.
88%of Australian adults aged 18–24 used 4 or more communication and social-media apps in the six months to June 2025, according to ACMA.

Sources: Ipsos iris, GenZ Category Report (September 2025), report PDF; ACMA, How we communicate — Executive summary and key findings (data to June 2025), ACMA. These statistics describe different populations and measures; they support social media as a relevant channel but do not guarantee campaign results.

How we measure success: track reach, video completion, profile visits, website menu clicks, offer redemptions and customer feedback. Compare results before and after launch to see which content brings people through the door.
