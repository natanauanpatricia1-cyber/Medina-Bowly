Our story · From the medina to your bowl

Tradition, with a fresh twist.

A medina is the historic heart of a Moroccan city — a lively maze of markets, craft, colour, aromas and food. That spirit inspires us: generous flavour, vibrant ingredients and food that brings people together.

We keep the soul. We rethink the plate.

Moroccan cuisine is rich in vegetables, pulses, grains, herbs and spice blends. Medina Bowl celebrates those ingredients while making practical changes to create lighter, balanced everyday meals.

Instead of deep-frying, we roast. Instead of relying on rich creamy sauces, we use Greek yoghurt, lemon and herbs. We choose wholemeal couscous and quinoa, include chickpeas and vegetables, and measure olive oil.

Our goal: food that feels nourishing and satisfying without losing the warmth, aroma and excitement of Moroccan-inspired cooking.

Nourishment, naturally

More than one kind of goodness.

We build variety into our menu so customers can enjoy a mix of nutrients across meals and drinks.

Protein

Chicken, chickpeas, quinoa and Greek yoghurt support growth, maintenance and muscle repair.

Fibre

Vegetables, legumes, fruit and whole grains support digestive health and help meals feel satisfying.

Healthy fats

Olive oil, tahini and seeds provide unsaturated fats that support normal body functions.

Vitamins

Capsicum, tomatoes, citrus, berries and leafy herbs contribute vitamins such as A, C and K.

Minerals

Chickpeas, greens, yoghurt and seeds contribute iron, calcium, potassium and magnesium.

Complex carbs

Wholemeal couscous and quinoa provide energy alongside fibre and other nutrients.

Hydration

Water-based citrus, mint and fruit drinks make hydration refreshing and enjoyable.

Antioxidants

Berries, pomegranate, tomatoes and herbs contain naturally occurring plant compounds.

Nutritional descriptions are ingredient-based guidance, not laboratory-tested nutrition claims. Exact values depend on portion sizes and preparation.
