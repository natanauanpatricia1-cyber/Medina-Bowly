The dish that tells our story

The Atlas
Chicken Bowl.

A fragrant, colourful bowl built from Moroccan-spiced grilled chicken, herby wholemeal couscous, roasted vegetables, smoky chickpeas and cool mint yoghurt.

◷ 40–50 minutes♨ Serves 4✦ High-protein concept

Serving inspiration: grilled chicken strips, couscous, chickpeas, roasted capsicum, zucchini, red onion, cherry tomatoes and mint yoghurt.

Ingredients

Measure and prepare each component before cooking.

ComponentIngredients & measurements
Chicken500 g chicken breast; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp cumin; 1 tsp paprika; ½ tsp coriander; ¼ tsp cinnamon; 1 garlic clove, crushed; juice of ½ lemon.
Couscous1½ cups wholemeal couscous; 1½ cups reduced-salt vegetable stock; ½ cup chopped parsley.
Vegetables1 red capsicum; 1 zucchini; 1 red onion; 1 cup cherry tomatoes; 1 tbsp olive oil.
Chickpeas1 × 400 g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed; ½ tsp cumin; ½ tsp paprika.
Mint yoghurt¾ cup plain Greek yoghurt; 2 tbsp chopped mint; juice of ½ lemon; 1–2 tsp water if needed.
To finishExtra mint or coriander leaves; lemon wedges; black pepper to taste.
Cook along · Step by step

From prep to plate.

Follow the sequence so the vegetables, chicken and couscous are ready together.

Marinate the chicken

Combine 1 tbsp olive oil, cumin, paprika, coriander, cinnamon, crushed garlic and lemon juice. Add chicken and coat all sides. Cover and refrigerate for at least 10 minutes while preparing vegetables.

Prepare the vegetables

Heat oven to 200°C (180°C fan-forced). Wash produce. Cut capsicum, zucchini and red onion into similar bite-sized pieces; leave cherry tomatoes whole.

Season vegetables & chickpeas

Drain and rinse chickpeas. Place vegetables and chickpeas on a lined tray. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, ½ tsp cumin and ½ tsp paprika. Spread in one layer.

Roast until tender

Roast for 20–25 minutes, turning halfway. Vegetables should be tender with lightly browned edges and chickpeas warmed and fragrant.

Cook chicken safely

Heat a non-stick pan or grill over medium-high heat. Cook chicken, turning as needed, until browned and cooked through. Check the thickest part reaches 75°C. Rest 5 minutes, then slice.

Prepare the couscous

Place couscous in a heatproof bowl. Bring stock to a boil, pour over couscous, cover and stand about 5 minutes (or follow packet directions). Fluff with a fork and fold through parsley.

Mix the mint yoghurt

Stir Greek yoghurt with chopped mint and lemon juice. Add 1–2 tsp water if needed to make a spoonable dressing. Season lightly with black pepper.

Build the bowls

Divide couscous between four bowls. Arrange sliced chicken, roasted vegetables and chickpeas in colourful sections on top. Add a spoonful of mint yoghurt.

Garnish & serve

Finish with fresh mint or coriander leaves and lemon wedges. Serve straight away so warm spices, roasted vegetables and cool yoghurt contrast beautifully.

Why this bowl is balanced: chicken, chickpeas and yoghurt contribute protein; wholemeal couscous, chickpeas and vegetables contribute fibre; olive oil supplies unsaturated fats; and colourful vegetables and herbs contribute a range of vitamins and minerals. Measured oil, roasting and a yoghurt-herb dressing keep the dish flavourful without relying on heavy sauces.
The nutrition story

Good ingredients, working together.

Protein supports growth, maintenance and muscle repair. Fibre supports digestive health. Unsaturated fats are part of a balanced diet, while whole grains provide energy and additional fibre. Vegetables and herbs add colour and a variety of micronutrients.
