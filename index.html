The recipe behind the favourite

The Atlas Chicken Bowl.

Our signature bowl combines bold Moroccan-inspired spices with fresh, nutrient-rich ingredients. A satisfying mix of lean protein, whole grains, legumes and colourful vegetables.

SERVES 4HIGH IN PROTEINSOURCE OF FIBRE

What's in it?

Chicken

  • 500 g chicken breast
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Cumin, paprika, coriander and cinnamon
  • 1 garlic clove
  • ½ lemon

Couscous

  • 1½ cups wholemeal couscous
  • 1½ cups reduced-salt vegetable stock
  • ½ cup parsley

Vegetables

  • 1 red capsicum
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Chickpeas

  • 1 × 400 g can chickpeas
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp paprika

Mint yoghurt

  • ¾ cup Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp fresh mint
  • ½ lemon
Let's make it

Method

Marinate

Combine chicken with olive oil, cumin, paprika, coriander, cinnamon, crushed garlic and lemon juice. Coat evenly and refrigerate for at least 10 minutes.

Prepare

Chop the capsicum, zucchini, red onion and cherry tomatoes into similar-sized pieces.

Roast

Toss vegetables and drained chickpeas with olive oil and spices. Roast at 200°C for approximately 20–25 minutes, turning halfway.

Cook the chicken

Cook the chicken in a hot pan or on a grill until completely cooked through, reaching an internal temperature of 75°C. Rest before slicing.

Prepare couscous

Pour hot vegetable stock over the couscous, cover for approximately 5 minutes, fluff with a fork and mix through parsley.

Make the dressing

Combine Greek yoghurt, chopped mint and lemon juice.

Assemble

Divide couscous between four bowls. Arrange chicken, roasted vegetables and chickpeas on top.

Garnish & serve

Finish with mint leaves, coriander and lemon wedges. Serve immediately.
